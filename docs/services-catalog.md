# Services Catalog — PsychAvenuePH

Source: `Site Map & Details` deck, Services Description slides (Images 5-6). Service descriptions only; no personal names or testimonial quotes.

## Consultation (45 mins)

- Initial support session for clients.
- Prior to meeting with their psychologist, clients may be asked to complete a brief set of screening tools, if necessary or upon request.
- Includes discussion of screening results, addressing client inquiries, and providing brief supplementary activities as appropriate.
- Guardrail: this is not a full therapy session but an initial step toward further psychological support.

## Individual Counseling & Psychotherapy (1 hr)

- Supportive space for clients to explore personal experiences and concerns.
- Psychologist collaborates with the client to identify therapeutic goals and develop a tailored treatment plan.
- Brief assessments or relevant psychotherapy worksheets/activities may be provided to enhance insight and facilitate progress.

## Couples Counseling (1.5 hr)

- Structured to support partners in exploring relationship concerns, communication patterns, goals, conflict resolution strategies, and other relevant issues.
- Advisable to allocate 15-minute individual breakout sessions with each partner, followed by a joint session for the remainder of the time.
- Therapeutic worksheets, activities, and treatment planning may be incorporated as needed; all interventions are tailored to the couple's unique dynamics and goals.

## Family / Group Counseling (2 hrs)

- Available based on the arrangements and clinical judgment of the assigned psychologist.
- Psychologists with training and/or specialization in family counseling have full autonomy to determine the most appropriate therapeutic approach and techniques suited to the needs of the group or family system.

## Life Coaching

- Designed to guide and support clients in completing therapy-related activities and assignments provided by their respective psychologists.
- Structured, brief, and solution-focused approach including goal setting and regular check-ins to promote accountability and progress.
- Guardrail: Life coaching is not a substitute for therapy.

## Clinical Supervision for Early Career Psychologists

- Supervision for newly registered psychologists as they navigate early clinical practice.
- Available to both practitioners affiliated with the clinic and those practicing externally.

## Academic & Research Services

- Expert support in thesis supervision and data analysis to guide students through their research journey.
- Guardrail: does not include taking full responsibility for writing an entire thesis report. The psychologist supports, mentors, and assists the student in understanding and completing their own work.
